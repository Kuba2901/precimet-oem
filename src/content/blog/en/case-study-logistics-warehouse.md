We partnered with one of Europe's largest logistics operators to equip a new distribution center. The order covered serial production of machine housings and load-bearing conveyor frames — components critical to internal material flow.

## The challenge

The client needed a single partner to handle the full chain: from process engineering through to delivery of finished modules on site. The deadline was fixed — hall commissioning was scheduled for a specific quarter. Documentation arrived in batches, with ongoing revisions. Dimensional repeatability across thousands of parts was non-negotiable; every millimetre of misalignment on a conveyor means a jammed system.

## The solution

We delivered the complete scope in-house:

**Laser cutting and bending** — structural grades S235 and S355, 3–12 mm thick. Our own material stock let us start production without waiting for deliveries.

**Structural welding** — machine housings and conveyor frames MIG-welded, with continuous welds in critical zones. Every part checked on fixtures.

**CNC milling and turning** — mounting holes, bearing seats, contact faces. Tolerances down to 0.1 mm on working elements.

**Sub-assembly** — finished modules: housings with pre-mounted shafts, bearings and seals; conveyor sections with guide profiles and drives. The client received plug-and-play modules — unload, position, connect power.

## Execution

1. **Documentation review and DFM feedback** — our CAD/CAM team verified drawings for manufacturability, proposed changes that simplified production without affecting function.
2. **Batched production** — weekly deliveries synchronized with the site installation schedule.
3. **Quality control** — incoming, in-process and final inspection per ISO 9001. Measurement reports included with every batch.
4. **Logistics** — packaged on returnable pallets, corrosion-protected, delivered in agreed time windows coordinated with the main contractor.

## Results

- Full production chain under one roof — no need to coordinate three separate subcontractors.
- Flexible response to drawing revisions mid-project.
- On-time delivery throughout the project.
- Client received finished modules that installed without on-site rework.
